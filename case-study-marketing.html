1. Strategic Audit & Keyword Taxonomy

The client was suffering from wasted ad budget on broad match keywords and inefficient landing page conversions. Fezhans performed a total audit, re-architecting exact-match SKAGs (Single Keyword Ad Groups) and deploying high-converting custom landing pages.

2. Execution & Multi-Funnel Retargeting

  • Google Search & Performance Max: Dominating top-of-page position 1 for high-intent transactional search queries.
  • Meta Video Ad Retargeting: Nurturing non-converting traffic with social proof video testimonials.
  • CRM Lead Nurturing: Automated email drip sequences turning trial signups into annual subscribers.

3. Scale & Revenue ROI

Client achieved a 10x MRR increase in 6 months while slashing customer acquisition cost (CAC) by 64%.
