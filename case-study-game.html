1. Game Design & Shader Optimization

The GOAT Digital Agency Game Studio designed and rendered full custom 3D low-poly models, custom HLSL shaders, and dynamic lighting rigs optimized to maintain 60 FPS across mid-tier mobile hardware.

By utilizing Unity’s Universal Render Pipeline (URP) and custom occlusion culling, memory consumption was kept below 180MB, resulting in near-zero crashes.

2. Monetization & Live Ops System

  • Hybrid Monetization: Seamless combination of In-App Purchases (gems, character skins) & non-intrusive rewarded video ads.
  • Real-Time Leaderboards: Redis-backed matchmaking infrastructure delivering instant global ranking updates.
  • Weekly Battle Passes: Automated cloud event updates driving long-term player retention.

3. Global Launch Success

The game achieved #1 Trending Game status on the Google Play Store in 14 countries within 3 weeks of release.
