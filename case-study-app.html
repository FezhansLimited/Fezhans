1. Product Vision & Architecture Roadmap

Space Alchemy required a flagship mobile application capable of executing real-time data syncs, high-fidelity dynamic UI animations, and secure biometric authentication across both Apple iOS App Store and Google Play platforms.

Fezhans engineered a unified cross-platform React Native codebase wrapped with native Swift and Kotlin native modules to guarantee 60fps native performance while reducing client development timeline by 45%.

2. Technical Execution Highlights

  • Native Bridge Acceleration: Custom Swift/Kotlin plugins for real-time camera processing and Bluetooth IoT connection.
  • Offline State Persistence: WatermelonDB & SQLite local cache allowing 100% offline usability with automated server reconciliations.
  • Biometric Security: Hardware-level iOS FaceID and Android Fingerprint encryption for user authentication.

3. Measurable Impact

Within 60 days of launch, Space Alchemy reached top 10 rankings in the App Store Productivity category with a 4.9 star customer rating across 12,000+ reviews.
